REFUND & CANCELLATION POLICY

This policy applies to Projection and Pro subscriptions and 7-day passes. It is part of our Terms of Service, and nothing in it limits your rights under the consumer laws of your country.

In short: Full refund within 14 days of your first monthly or yearly payment (once per account); a renewal within 7 days of the charge; an upgrade charge within 7 days of the upgrade, which puts the plan back on Projection; after that, a yearly plan refunds the unused days. A 7-day pass, including one that has not started yet, can be refunded during its 7 days: in full if you have not used it, otherwise for the days you have not used, counted by day.

This policy is published in English.

Effective date: November 9, 2026

REFUND & CANCELLATION POLICY

  1. (01)

    Cancel any time

    Open Account settings in the app and choose Cancel subscription. It takes one click and one confirmation. You keep access until the end of the period you have paid for, and you will not be charged again. Cancelling stops future charges; a refund is a separate request, described below. A 7-day pass needs no cancelling: it ends by itself 7 days after you buy it.

  3. (03)

    Trials and launch benefits

    The 21-day Pro trial, the 30 days of free Pro for accounts created before paid plans start, and the 12 months of Pro for Founding Members need no card and never turn into a paid plan by themselves. Nothing is charged, so there is nothing to refund.

  4. (04)

    How to ask for a refund

    Email info@field-lux.com from your account email, or reply to your Paddle receipt, and tell us which payment you mean; the order number on the receipt helps. You can use the form below, but you do not have to.

    We process every refund within 3 business days of your request. Paddle.com, our Merchant of Record, returns the money to the payment method you used, including the tax you paid on it. Your bank or card issuer may take a few more days to show it.

  5. (05)

    Paddle's refund policy

    Payments are processed by Paddle.com, which acts as the Merchant of Record for all our orders, and Paddle's own refund policy also applies to purchases. Where this policy or the law gives you more, the more generous terms apply. We carry out the refunds promised here ourselves, through Paddle, and we stay responsible for them.
