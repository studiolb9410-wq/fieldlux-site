SEE EVERY TOOL DO ITS ACTUAL JOB.

One short clip per feature, recorded in the shipped app. No narration, no speed-up.

Every part of the product gets a short screen recording, filed under the same ten chapters the home page uses. Chapters fill in as each clip is shot.

A dashed card is not decoration. It is a feature that is documented but not yet filmed: its title, its chapter and its description are final, and the running time beside it is the plan for the clip. Only the recording is outstanding, so this index reads complete today and gains motion as clips land.
