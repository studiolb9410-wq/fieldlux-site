PRIVACY POLICY

This policy explains what personal information FieldLux collects, why, how long we keep it, who processes it for us and where, and what you can ask of us. It covers this website, field-lux.com, and the app at app.field-lux.com.

This policy is published in English.

Effective date: November 9, 2026

Change posted November 10, 2026, effective January 1, 2027: how long uploaded files of unused accounts are kept, in section 04.

PRIVACY POLICY

  1. (01)

    Who is responsible

    [Business name] (representative: [Representative], [Business address], business registration no. [Registration no.]) runs FieldLux and is responsible for your personal information. Our privacy officer is named in section 13.

  2. (02)

    This website

    These pages are static files with no account, no form, no cookies and no analytics, tag manager or tracking script. The stylesheet, fonts and images come from field-lux.com itself, so reading this site sends no request to a third party.

    Your browser keeps a few keys for this site: fieldlux_motion when you press Reduce motion, fieldlux_pricing_period when you open the pricing page or switch its billing period, fieldlux_beta_notice_until when you choose "Don't show again today" on the open-beta notice (it holds that day's date) and fieldlux_beta_notice_closed for the rest of your visit when you close that notice or follow its link, and two that exist only for review builds and are written only if you open the site with the matching parameter: fieldlux_flow_app_url for ?flowAppUrl=, kept for that browser tab only and accepting nothing but a field-lux.com app address, and fieldlux_discord_url for ?discordUrl=, which accepts nothing but a Discord link and is kept until you clear this site's data. The 3D demo on the home page is the FieldLux app served from this domain at /embed; it may keep its own view preferences, named with a fieldlux_ prefix, in the same storage. None of these keys is sent anywhere, and clearing this site's data removes them.

    The demo frame shares this page's origin, so it is not a separate security boundary. It is sandboxed without forms or top-level navigation and contacts no other site. Sign in and Start free take you to app.field-lux.com with one parameter, siteReturnUrl, the page you came from. The Discord link opens discord.gg. Vercel, our host, records the requests it serves, such as network address, browser and time, as any web host does; we keep no log of our own.

  5. (05)

    Payments

    Paid plans are sold by Paddle.com Market Limited (United Kingdom), which acts as the Merchant of Record. Paddle collects your name, email, billing country, payment details and tax information to process payments, taxes and invoices under its own privacy notice. We receive your plan, subscription status, renewal and end dates, country and Paddle customer and subscription IDs. We never receive your full card number. Because Paddle uses this information for its own purposes as the seller, we ask for your consent at checkout before your details go to Paddle.

  7. (07)

    Email we send

    We do not sell personal information. We send product news and offers only to people who opt in, and you can opt out at any time in Account settings or from the link in any such email. Account, security, billing and service notices go to every account.

  8. (08)

    Browser storage and analytics in the app

    The app keeps your sign-in session, your preferences and the random visitor ID in your browser's local storage. It sets no advertising cookies, and Vercel Web Analytics works without cookies. To turn usage and error reports off, use the "Share usage and error reports" switch under Privacy choices on the sign-in screen or in Account settings; turning it off stops the usage events, error reports and Vercel Web Analytics and Speed Insights for that browser. If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track signal, reports start off. Clearing the app's site data removes everything it stored in that browser.

  9. (09)

    Your rights

    You can ask to see, correct, delete or export your personal information, ask us to stop processing it, or withdraw a consent. Email jay@field-lux.com from your account email; we reply within 10 days. A legal representative can make these requests for you. You can export any project yourself with File, then Export Project File, and delete your account in Account settings. Where the law makes us keep something, such as billing records, we tell you so.

  10. (10)

    How we delete it

    When a retention period ends or its purpose is met, we delete electronic records so they cannot be restored. We keep no paper records. Records the law requires us to keep are stored apart from other data and used only for that purpose.

  11. (11)

    How we protect it

    Connections use TLS. Passwords are handled only by Supabase Auth and stored hashed, and share-link passwords are hashed too. Network addresses are stored only as keyed hashes. Row-level security limits each account to its own data, and administrator access is limited to the operator.

  12. (12)

    Children

    FieldLux is not for children under 14, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If we learn that we have, we delete it.
