PRIVACY POLICY

This policy explains what personal information FieldLux collects, why, how long we keep it, who processes it for us and where, and what you can ask of us. It covers this website, field-lux.com, and the app at app.field-lux.com.

This policy is published in English.

Effective date: November 9, 2026

Change posted November 10, 2026, effective January 1, 2027: how long uploaded files of unused accounts are kept, in section 04.

Wording revised [Posted date]: sections 02, 03, 04, 06, 08 and 11 now say the same things in plain language. Nothing changes in what we collect, why, how long we keep it or who receives it.

  1. (01)

    Who is responsible

    [Business name] (representative: [Representative], [Business address], business registration no. [Registration no.]) runs FieldLux and is responsible for your personal information. Our privacy officer is named in section 13.

  2. (02)

    This website

    This website, field-lux.com, has no accounts and no forms, and it sets no cookies. It runs no analytics, advertising or tracking tools. Its text, styles, fonts and images all come from field-lux.com itself, so reading these pages makes your browser contact no other website.

    To keep the pages the way you left them, your browser remembers a few settings for this site. They stay in your browser: none of them is sent to us or to anyone else, and clearing this site's data in your browser removes them all.

    What your browser remembersWhenKept for
    Your motion choice: whether pages play their animationsWhen you press Reduce motion or Enable motion at the bottom of a pageUntil you clear this site's data
    The billing period you last viewed on the Pricing page: 7-day pass, monthly or yearlyWhen you open the Pricing page or switch its billing periodUntil you clear this site's data

    For our own testing only: a different invite for the Discord links, which must be a Discord linkOnly if you open this site through a special link we use to check new versions of the siteUntil you clear this site's data

    The 3D demo on the home page is the FieldLux app itself, running on this site. Because it runs on the same site, it shares the browser storage described above and may remember its own view settings there, which are not sent anywhere either. The demo cannot send forms or take you to another page, and it contacts no other website.

    The Sign in and Start free buttons take you to the app at app.field-lux.com and tell it only which page of this site you came from, so it can bring you back. The Discord links open Discord's website, discord.gg.

    Vercel, the company that hosts this site, records the requests it serves, such as your network address, your browser and the time, as any web host does. We keep no records of our own about visits to this site.

  5. (05)

    Payments

    Paid plans are sold by Paddle.com Market Limited (United Kingdom), which acts as the Merchant of Record. Paddle collects your name, email, billing country, payment details and tax information to process payments, taxes and invoices under its own privacy notice. We receive your plan, subscription status, renewal and end dates, country and Paddle customer and subscription IDs. We never receive your full card number. Because Paddle uses this information for its own purposes as the seller, we ask for your consent at checkout before your details go to Paddle.

  7. (07)

    Email we send

    We do not sell personal information. We send product news and offers only to people who opt in, and you can opt out at any time in Account settings or from the link in any such email. Account, security, billing and service notices go to every account.

  8. (08)

    Browser storage and analytics in the app

    The app keeps what it needs to keep you signed in, your preferences and the random visitor ID in your browser's storage. It sets no advertising cookies, and Vercel's visitor measurement works without cookies. To turn usage and error reports off, use the "Share usage and error reports" switch under Privacy choices on the sign-in screen or in Account settings; turning it off stops the usage record, error reports and Vercel's visitor and page-speed measurement for that browser. If your browser is set to send a privacy signal asking sites not to track you (Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track), reports start off. Clearing the app's site data in your browser removes everything it stored there.

  9. (09)

    Your rights

    You can ask to see, correct, delete or export your personal information, ask us to stop processing it, or withdraw a consent. Email jay@field-lux.com from your account email; we reply within 10 days. A legal representative can make these requests for you. You can export any project yourself with File, then Export Project File, and delete your account in Account settings. Where the law makes us keep something, such as billing records, we tell you so.

  10. (10)

    How we delete it

    When a retention period ends or its purpose is met, we delete electronic records so they cannot be restored. We keep no paper records. Records the law requires us to keep are stored apart from other data and used only for that purpose.

  11. (11)

    How we protect it

    All connections are encrypted. Passwords are handled only by our sign-in provider, Supabase, and are stored only in a scrambled form that cannot be turned back into the password; share-link passwords are stored the same way. Network addresses are stored only in a coded form made with a secret key, never as the address itself. Rules in the database itself limit each account to its own data, and only the operator has administrator access.

  12. (12)

    Children

    FieldLux is not for children under 14, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If we learn that we have, we delete it.
