THE WORKFLOW

BUILD THE VENUE. PLACE THE SYSTEM. SHARE THE PLAN.

Two workspaces share one scene. Design holds the venue; Simulate holds the projectors, lighting and blend. Coverage and measurement readouts follow the geometry in front of you.

01 / DESIGN

Build the venue

Import a 3D model (OBJ, FBX, GLB, glTF, DAE, STL, PLY) with unit detection and a one-click up-axis fix, load a DWG, DXF, PDF or image floor plan at scale, trace its walls and raise them, or start from a Basic Room, Flat Surface, Curved Wall, Cylinder, Dome or a site photo. Everything sits at real scale in one scene.

02 / PROJECTOR LIBRARY

Choose the projector and lens

Pick an Epson, Panasonic or Christie head and a lens from its compatible list, or build a custom head from any spec sheet and save it for reuse. Epson and Panasonic lux readouts are cross-checked against the manufacturers' own calculators.

03 / PLACEMENT TOOL

Place, aim and blend the rig

Set throw ratio and lens shift inside the lens's real range, then build linear, circular or symmetry arrays that stay live as you move the source. Group the heads, run Auto-Blend, and set the overlap per edge in percent and metres.

04 / HEATMAP

Read the surface

Turn on the Heatmap overlay for Surface Lux, Pixel Density or Luminance, press Values for the 13pt Diagnostic, and check the projection-to-ambient contrast ratio. Every readout is a physics estimate, labelled as one.

05 / PLAY DOCK

Preview the content

Open the Play Dock, let Output Setup find the screens from your beams, drop images or video from the Media Library, and corner-pin, mesh-warp and soft-edge each head in Warp & Blend. Play Dock previews the show in 3D. It does not output to hardware.

06 / SHARE AND HANDOFF

Hand off the plan

Export a PDF Tech Rider in one click, save an editable .flx.json project file, or create a read-only Cloud Share link with a QR code, an optional password and an expiry, and revoke it at any time. Projects are cloud-saved with autosave and version history.

LIGHTING AND ATMOSPHERE

PUT THE FIXTURES AND THE FOG IN THE SAME SCENE.

Plan projection, lighting and atmosphere in the same venue. The lighting rig adds visual context without changing the projector lux calculation.

FIXTURES

Eight fixture types, or your own

Add a Moving Head Spot, Wash or Beam, LED PAR, Profile, Fresnel, Flood or Strobe from the Lighting menu, pick a catalogue class, or import GDTF and MVR files. Load IES or LDT photometry for the real beam. Tune one and save it to My Fixtures to reuse it in other projects.

WORKING PLANE

Isolux on a plane you set

Show the isolux working plane at any height, read average, minimum, maximum and uniformity, save fixture states as Looks, and copy the fixture schedule as CSV for the rider.

FOG

Beams you can see in the air

Add a Fog Machine, set output, fluid and plume, then dial wind, gust and ventilation under Appearance. Projector and fixture beams glow in the fog, up to four of each at a time. Measured lux does not change with fog.

READ WHAT LANDS

SURFACE LUX, PIXEL DENSITY AND LUMINANCE, ON THE MODEL.

Compare Absolute, Norm and Deviation maps, probe any point under the cursor, and read one head or the composite directly on the projected surfaces in the scene.
