THE WORKFLOW

BUILD THE VENUE. PLACE THE SYSTEM. SHARE THE PLAN.

Start with the venue, keep the optics tied to the rig, then share a plan your team can review.

01 / SIMULATION

Build the venue

Import a 3D model or floor plan, draw the room, and see coverage update on the venue geometry.

02 / PROJECTOR LIBRARY

Choose the projector and lens

Choose a projector and compatible lens, or add a custom head. Source references stay attached to each catalogue entry.

03 / PLACEMENT TOOL

Place and aim the rig

Set throw ratio and lens shift, then build linear, circular, or mirrored arrays. Every move updates the beam and coverage.

04 / PLAY DOCK

Preview mapped content

Use the Mapping Stage to place images or video on a surface and preview playback in 3D. Play Dock does not control or output the live show.

05 / DELIVERABLES

Hand off the plan

Send a PDF Tech Rider, editable FieldLux project file, or read-only link. The result remains a simulation, not a field measurement.
