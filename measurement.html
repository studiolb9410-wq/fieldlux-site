WHAT YOU CAN READ

THREE VALUES. THREE DIFFERENT QUESTIONS.

Choose the value that fits the decision. Lux, image scale, and surface brightness are not interchangeable.

SURFACE LUX

How much light lands here?

Read estimated illuminance in lx, with contributing projectors combined at the sampled point.

PIXEL DENSITY

How large is each pixel?

Read image scale on the target in mm/px, so resolution is tied to the real surface.

LUMINANCE

How bright does the surface appear?

Read estimated surface brightness in cd/m2, with the selected material kept in the calculation.

WHAT THE NUMBERS MEAN

A PLANNING ESTIMATE, WITH ITS BASIS SHOWN.

FieldLux calculates from published projector and lens data plus the geometry and settings in your scene. It is an optics-based planning estimate, not a lux-meter reading.
