WHO THIS IS FOR

FieldLux answers how many heads, which lens, from where and how bright before anyone signs, and it stops there: not a media server, not a rig warping tool, not a photoreal renderer, and nothing authored in it is ever sent to a device.

THE PRODUCT, PART BY PART

Ten parts, and every one of them opens in the deployed web app today, so where a part is gated, still in beta, or desktop-only, this page says so in the same sentence that claims it.

(01) THE SCENE

One scene holds the building, the heads, the lens on each and the surfaces you must not spill onto, and every number downstream recomputes from it, and it arrives as one of six model formats or is built from the rooms, curves and domes placed here, because FieldLux does not draw the venue for you.

(02) THE OPTICS

Every optical number comes off a manufacturer spec sheet, 87 projectors and 113 lenses deep, each field carrying the URL of the PDF it was read from, and a head placed outside its catalogue throw envelope has its beam and lux cards struck through rather than printing a figure it cannot reach.

(03) THE COVERAGE

Auto edge blend measures the footprints the beams actually make and writes per-edge overlap widths and one blend curve in a single undo step, and those are light-linear planning values for the study, never settings pushed to a processor, which is why every export that carries them states the domain they are in.

(04) THE CORRECTION

Corner-pin, mesh warp and feathered masks render in the actual 3D beam rather than a flat panel, so the seam softens in the viewport while you drag it, and that is where it stops: your on-site correction still happens on the processor, and 16 heads is the cap on how many can carry a warp map or mask.

(05) THE CONTENT

Drag the image in 2D and the beams follow in 3D, with the parts that fall outside the canvas shown as cut, inside a budget of four distinct content sources, where the fifth drop is refused out loud before anything is written rather than failing silently on the GPU.

(06) THE MEASUREMENT

The surface becomes a light meter: false-colour maps in three data modes and three scales, a probe under the cursor, and 13 points drawn on the target in world space, and every figure is computed from the configured projector specs and the scene geometry, never from a field measurement, with the error each assumption carries set out under Limits.

(07) THE LIGHT

Projector light composites additively onto the surface's own shading, so a white beam brightens a dark wall while its texture stays visible, and the model stops at direct light plus a single-bounce room estimate carrying the widest error budget in the product, judged against ambient presets from 100,000 lx direct sunlight down to 5 lx theatre and full blackout.

(08) THE ROOM AROUND IT

Stage and architectural fixtures and LED walls sit in the same scene as the projectors, eight archetypes deep with IES (LM-63) import and a washout rating for the wall, in a strictly separate estimate lane, direct light only with no interreflection, stamped estimate in code and on screen, and blocked from ever reaching the projector lux figure.

(09) THE DELIVERABLE

Three things leave the app: a PDF Tech Rider carrying its printed disclaimer band, a vendor-neutral handoff JSON, and a read-only link that is a dated receipt, not a live mirror, each one a document a person reads or a machine parses, none of it written in a media server's own blend or warp format or pushed to a device.

(10) THE PROJECT

A local crash draft, a cloud autosave on an append-only version chain and a restore that deletes nothing mean the work survives the session, and where a project cannot be fully revived the app refuses to write rather than overwrite your last good snapshot, which is still not an offline mode: signing in needs the network.

Six figures, each one read off the shipped build or off a session someone sat through and counted, each naming its own source on its own line, and each dated where a date is what makes it true.

A hard GPU cap, not a plan tier. The runtime detector can set it lower on your machine, and 16 of those heads can carry a mesh warp or a mask.

PROBE GRID POINTS

13

9 ANSI plus 4 quarter-points, drawn on the target. A separate ring of 16 outer-edge probes covers the perimeter, and surface sampling is a 16 x 16 grid.

REQUESTS WHILE YOU WORK

0

Moving, rotating and editing values issue no network request. The server is touched at cold load, sign-in, project listing and open/import/save: a measured one-hour session came to about 55 requests.

LIMITS

Every constant the analysis runs on is frozen in one module with the industry source it came from and the error it carries, and they are published here because a field engineer should be able to read a number off this product and know exactly what was assumed to produce it.

(01) WHAT IS ASSUMED, AND WHAT EACH ASSUMPTION COSTS

ROOM BOUNCE

Single-bounce form factor, a heuristic rather than a standard. Error budget +/- 50 %, and the code says in as many words that it is for planning, not measurement. It is the worst budget in this table, which is why it is stated first.

SURFACE REFLECTANCE

0.60, ISO 9241-303 reference matte white. Error budget +/- 0.10 absolute, ordinary paint variance. If you know the paint that is actually on the surface, set a material preset instead.

PROJECTOR UNIFORMITY

ANSI 9-point: centre 1.00, edge 0.90, corner 0.85, average 0.92. Source ANSI/INFOCOMM 2M-2010. Error budget +/- 5 % per point while the head is inside its spec, +/- 15 % once it is outside.

SURFACE SAMPLING

16 x 16 = 256 points across the target. Error budget +/- 5 % on Reliable Surface Lux (full-target p20), falling to +/- 1 % at 32 x 32. The shipped grid is the 16 x 16 one.

THROW GEOMETRY

Raycast from the optical frame to the target. Error budget +/- 2 % on flat targets, +/- 15 % on imported models. On a model you did not build yourself, work to the +/- 15 % figure.

NON-PLANAR FALLBACK

Bounding-box centroid plane projection. Error budget +/- 15 to 30 %. It flattens the curve into a plane to get a number at all, so on a curved target carry the whole band, not the middle of it.

HOW THESE COMBINE

They do not. Each budget is declared on its own, and neither the app nor this page rolls them into a single accuracy figure for a run. A curved imported target lit through bounced light sits inside three of these bands at once, and how they interact is not modelled. Read them as separate warnings, not as one number to subtract.

(02) WHAT IS NOT MODELLED, ABSENT RATHER THAN APPROXIMATE

NOT MODELLED

Atmospheric haze, lens distortion, chromatic aberration, lamp aging, specular BRDF, ambient interaction with the surface, content-dependent brightness. Surfaces are Lambertian only. There is no radiosity, no global illumination and no ray tracing anywhere in this product. These do not tighten with a longer run; they are absent from the calculation.

FIXTURE LIGHT IS A SEPARATE LANE

Stage and architectural fixture illuminance is stamped "estimate" in code and on screen: direct light only, no interreflection. That module is blocked from importing the projector photometry code at all, and a test checks its import list stays clean every run. A context estimate can never mix into the projector lux figure.

DIALUX-STYLE, NOT DIALUX-GRADE

The working-plane isolux calculation is described as DIALux-style. It is not a certified calculation and it is not a substitute for one.

IES CAVEAT

Asymmetric IES files are averaged into an axially symmetric approximation, and the app says so on screen. A fixture whose real distribution is not symmetric about its axis is estimated as though it were.

(03) WHAT IS NOT CLAIMED

NO MEASURED VALIDATION

Nothing in this product has been compared against lux-meter field data. The budgets above are self-declared model uncertainty, not a measured accuracy figure. Every map carries its accuracy basis on its own legend: Physics Model (estimated), Spec-based (datasheet), Calibrated or Measured. We say "simulation preview" and "physics estimate" because that is what it is.

WHICH LUX, ALWAYS LABELLED

Four lux figures with different meanings exist in the engine. Reliable Surface Lux is a full-target 20th percentile. Not a peak, not an average. Every lux number on this site says which one it is in the same sentence, and quoting one without its label is how the same scene ends up with two brightness stories.

BLEND VALUES ARE LIGHT-LINEAR

FieldLux blend ramps are a partition of unity in luminous flux, and every export carrying them states that domain. Typed verbatim into a signal-space media-server blend panel they put roughly a -56 % dark band at seam centre. They are planning values, not processor settings.

NEVER UNDER LOAD

This platform has never been load tested. The highest traffic we have observed is 30 requests per second. We publish the measured per-session request cost and nothing else: no uptime figure, no concurrency guarantee.

THE PUBLIC-BUCKET CAVEAT

Model binaries and site photos referenced by a share are served from a public storage bucket. Anyone who has already resolved a share can keep downloading those files after you revoke, expire or password-protect the link. This is known, documented and deliberately deferred: revocation stops the link, it does not un-download a file.

WHAT'S IN BETA

GDTF and MVR fixture import is code-complete but not yet checked against real vendor files. The Report Builder client-proposal PDF sits behind a beta gate and is off in deployed builds. Both are labelled that way inside the app too.

WHAT ISN'T HERE

Drawing import and sketch-to-model are disabled in production. Realtime video output to other applications is desktop-only and its entry points are removed from the web build entirely. There is no export to a media-server-specific file format. There is no live multi-user editing, no comments and no presence. Sharing is owner-authored links plus read-only account access.

SECURITY, STATED NARROWLY

What exists: row-level security scoped to the project owner, share payloads readable only through a server-side resolver, bcrypt-hashed share passwords, IP-hashed rate limiting on share resolution, per-user storage path isolation, admin-gated operations, a 7-day grace period on account deletion, and an explicit consent record at signup. That list is the whole claim.
