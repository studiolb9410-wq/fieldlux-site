What is in the box

Projection, lighting and content, in one scene.

Eight things the app does today, all free during the open beta. Each one has a chapter on the Videos page with the exact clicks.

01 · Venue

Bring the venue in.

Import an OBJ, FBX, GLB, glTF, DAE, STL or PLY model and FieldLux reads its unit and up axis. Load a DWG, DXF, PDF or image floor plan at scale and raise its walls with one key, or sketch the room from scratch. Need a stand-in: a Basic Room, Flat Surface, Curved Wall, Cylinder, Dome, a site photo or an LED wall is one click under Tools.

How to do it: Chapters 02 to 04

02 · Projector Library

Real heads, real lenses.

Pick an Epson, Panasonic or Christie projector and a lens from the list its manufacturer documents for it. Epson and Panasonic lux readouts are cross-checked against the manufacturers' own calculators. Anything else becomes a custom head from its spec sheet, saved for the next project.

How to do it: Chapter 05

03 · Placement Tool

Arrays that stay live.

Build a linear run, a circular ring with a shared aim, or a symmetry pair, and watch the ghost preview and coordinate table before you bake. Start Live and every clone follows the source when you move it. Match position or rotation across heads with one click.

How to do it: Chapter 07

04 · Edge blending

Seams found for you.

Group the heads that overlap, press Auto-Blend, and FieldLux measures where the beams meet on the surface and sets each edge in percent and metres. Pick Linear, Gamma 2.2 or S-Curve, mirror a feather to the partner, match brightness across the seam.

Measured lux stays pre-blend: the map shows each head at full white so the plan never hides a hot spot.

How to do it: Chapter 08

05 · Heatmap

Know the lux before the truck leaves.

Turn on the Heatmap overlay for Surface Lux, Pixel Density or Luminance. Read it on an Absolute, Norm or Deviation scale, press Values for the 13pt Diagnostic with a uniformity grade, probe any point under the cursor, and set the venue's ambient light to read the contrast ratio.

FieldLux is a planning estimate, not a calibrated field measurement. The Heatmap panel labels the basis of every map: Physics Model, Spec-based or Calibrated.

How to do it: Chapters 09 to 11

06 · Play Dock

Put the content on the wall.

Output Setup finds the screens from where your beams land. Drop images or video from the Media Library, build a playlist, and corner-pin, mesh-warp, mask and soft-edge each head in Warp & Blend, all rendered live on the 3D model.

Play Dock previews the show. It does not output to hardware.

How to do it: Chapters 12 and 13

07 · Lighting and fog

The rest of the rig, in the same scene.

Add moving heads, PARs, profiles, Fresnels, floods or strobes, import GDTF, MVR, IES and LDT files, and read an isolux working plane at any height. Add a Fog Machine and the projector and fixture beams glow in the air.

Fixtures are a context layer. They never touch the projector lux formula.

How to do it: Chapters 14 and 15

08 · Share and handoff

One link for the client, one PDF for the crew.

Create a Cloud Share link with a QR code, optional password and expiry, choose what viewers can do, and revoke it from Share Management. Export a PDF Tech Rider in one click, or an editable .flx.json project file. Projects are cloud-saved with autosave and version history.

How to do it: Chapters 16 to 18
