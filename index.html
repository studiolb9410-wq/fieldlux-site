Hover measurements

Measure the surface under your cursor, not after export.

FieldLux shows point-level lux and pixel density directly in the viewport. The probe follows the lit receiver surface, ignores unlit or occluded areas, and reveals overlap contribution when multiple projectors stack.

Lux at point Total lux at the cursor
Pixel density mm/px at the same hit point
