PRICING

THE PRICES ARE SET. THE CHECKOUT IS NOT BUILT.

This page publishes what FieldLux will cost and how it will work. It is not a store. There is no purchase screen in the app, no payment code in the build and no plan enforcement anywhere in it, so nothing here can be bought today and no button on this page charges anything. Every FieldLux account right now is open beta and free.

It is published this early for one reason. Somebody deciding whether to build a way of working on a tool needs the number before the workflow is built, not after it. Every figure here is stated with what it counts, the arithmetic that put it where it is gets printed rather than left for you to do, and where something is a decision rather than a shipped behaviour the row says which of the two it is.

Published: September 6, 2026. The prices below are proposed. No account has been charged.

WHAT AN EXPIRED PASS KEEPS

TODAY

TODAY IT IS FREE, AND NOT AS A TRIAL.

Every account on FieldLux is an open beta account. There is no card on file, no clock counting down to a paywall and no tier to choose at signup. The three rows below are the only limits on this page that anything currently enforces, and they are running in the deployed app as you read this.

IMPORTED MODEL STORAGE

2 GB per account, across every project you own, with a 200 MB ceiling on any single model file. Both are open beta quotas and both are enforced in the app today.

MEDIA LIBRARY

1 GiB per account. That is an open beta quota and the app holds you to it today; a single media file is bounded by the account quota and nothing tighter.

NOTHING IS BEHIND A PAYMENT

No part of the product sits behind a payment gate, because there is no payment gate to sit behind. Where something is switched off it is switched off by a beta flag, and the app says so on the row that carries it.

THE THREE PASSES

ONE WEEK, ONE MONTH, OR ONE YEAR.

Three lengths, each bought once, each in Korean won and each including VAT. There is no feature ladder between them: the only difference between the three rows below is how many days. Which one is cheaper for the way you actually work is arithmetic rather than opinion, so it is worked out in the next section instead of argued for here.

WEEK PASS, 7 DAYS

KRW 9,900 including VAT, paid once. Sized to one job rather than one month: buy it in the week you are quoting or rigging, and buy nothing in the weeks you are not.

MONTH PASS, 30 DAYS

KRW 17,900 including VAT, paid once. For a month with more than one job in it.

YEAR PASS, 365 DAYS

KRW 149,000 including VAT, paid once. That is about KRW 12,400 for each month of it, and it is the row for a designer who has FieldLux open most weeks of the year rather than a few.

WHAT A PASS BUYS

The whole app, in one state. No head-count tier, no export held back for the longer pass and no published limit that moves for money. A pass buys days, and that is the only thing it buys.

THE ARITHMETIC

EVERY CROSSOVER, WORKED OUT.

Five comparisons, and three of them say buy the smaller thing. The ladder was set so that you cannot overpay by buying in the small pieces that suit the way you work, and the only way to show that is to print the sums.

ONE JOB IN A MONTH

9,900 as a week pass against 17,900 as a month pass. The week pass is 8,000 cheaper. If you open FieldLux once in a month, that is the row.

TWO JOBS IN THE SAME MONTH

19,800 as two week passes against 17,900 as one month pass. The month pass is 1,900 cheaper. This is the crossover the ladder was set around, and it sits at the second purchase rather than the fourth, which was the one deliberate correction made to these numbers.

TWO JOBS, TWO DIFFERENT MONTHS

19,800 as two week passes against 35,800 as two month passes. The week passes are 16,000 cheaper. Two jobs in a year is not two jobs in a month, and the crossover above does not carry across a gap.

FIFTEEN WEEKS OF USE

148,500 as fifteen week passes against 149,000 as a year pass. 500 apart. Below fifteen weeks the week passes cost less, above it the year does, and at fifteen the two are the same money.

A FULL YEAR

149,000 as a year pass, 214,800 as twelve month passes, 514,800 as fifty-two week passes. Against month passes the year pays for itself in the ninth month: eight of them come to 143,200 and nine come to 161,100.

WHEN A PASS ENDS

NOTHING IS DELETED. NOTHING IS LOCKED AWAY.

This is the part worth reading before the prices, because it is what makes a seven-day pass a sane thing to buy. When a pass runs out the account changes what it will let you do next. It does not change what it will let you reach. The working name for that state is delivery mode.

(01) WHAT AN EXPIRED ACCOUNT KEEPS

OPENING AND VIEWING

Every project on the account still opens and still loads in 3D. Projectors, lenses, masks, materials and saved measurement results are all still there to look at, on a screen, in front of a client.

A SHARE LINK YOU ALREADY MADE

A read-only share link you have not revoked, expired or password-protected keeps resolving. A pass ending does not close a link a client is already using. What a share does and does not protect does not change either way.

THE PDF TECH RIDER

The PDF Tech Rider export keeps working. A document you owe somebody can still leave the app after the pass that produced it has ended.

SHOWING IT ON A SCREEN

Delivery mode adds nothing to stop you. The viewport is not blanked, watermarked or covered over when a pass ends, and no measurement label is hidden that was visible the day before.

YOUR FILES

Imported models and media stay on the account under the same quotas as before. Expiry is not a deletion event. What happens to an account that has been dormant for a very long time is not decided, and it is left off this page rather than guessed at.

(02) WHAT AN EXPIRED ACCOUNT STOPS

NEW PROJECTS

Creating a new project needs a live pass.

EDITING AND SAVING

Changing anything inside a project, and saving that change, needs a live pass. What is already saved stays exactly as saved, and buying another pass returns the account to editing with the same projects in it.

(03) AND NONE OF IT IS RUNNING

NOTHING CAN EXPIRE YET

No pass exists, so no pass can run out. Delivery mode is specified and has never run: no account has been in it and none can be put in it. Every account today is in full edit with no pass at all.

THE FINE PRINT

HOW THE MONEY WOULD WORK.

Written in the conditional throughout, because none of it is live. These are the terms a first charge would be made under, published now so that they cannot be quietly written differently once there is something to charge for.

NOT BUILT

No checkout, no payment code, no plan enforcement. The app today has one state, open beta, and it is free for everyone in it. Nothing on this page can be bought and nobody has been charged.

SINGLE PAYMENTS ONLY

Each pass is one payment for one period. No card is stored, nothing renews on its own, and there is no subscription to remember to cancel because there is no subscription. A pass ends by ending, and buying another one is something you do on purpose.

CHOOSING THE START DATE

A pass can be dated forward at purchase, up to 14 days out. Buy on the Monday for a pass that opens on the Thursday, so a week pass bought for a show does not expire the night of the show. Designed, not built.

CURRENCY

Charged in Korean won through a Korean payment provider. TossPayments is the plan, not an integration. This page prints no second currency and no approximate conversion: a rate written here today is wrong by the time it is read, and your card issuer converts at a rate we do not set and cannot quote.

TAX

The three figures include VAT. The price you read is the price that would be charged, and there is no amount added at the end.

REFUND, PASS NOT STARTED

A pass bought and not yet started refunds in full.

REFUND, PASS RUNNING

A pass that has started refunds pro rata on the days remaining. A month pass cancelled on day 10 refunds the other 20 days.

FOUNDING MEMBERS

Early testers who verify their invite code keep founding-member standing, which carries 12 months of access once paid plans begin. That is the same promise the home page already makes, and nothing on this page shortens it.

NO RETROACTIVE CHARGE

No account is billed for time already spent in the open beta. Whatever the first charge turns out to be, it starts on the day plans begin and not on the day you signed up.

WHAT A PASS WOULD NOT CHANGE

No published limit moves for money. The ceiling of 200 projectors in a scene is a hard shader cap and the runtime detector can set it lower on your machine; no pass raises it. The 2 GB model store, the 1 GiB media library and the 200 MB per-model-file ceiling are the same on every pass, and no pass changes how a number is calculated.

WHAT A FREE ACCOUNT IS AFTER THAT

Not decided. Whether an account with no pass can still be created once paid plans begin has not been settled, and it is left off this page rather than guessed at. It lands here in writing before it lands in the app.

IF THESE NUMBERS MOVE

This page carries the date it was published, and the date moves when the text does. There is no mailing list to announce a change on, and there is no form on this page to leave an address in, which is exactly why the date sits under the title where you can see it.

NOT BUILT

NOTHING HERE CAN BE BOUGHT.

This site ships a rule: a claim only stands if a visitor could reach the thing it describes in the deployed build today. A price for something nobody can buy fails that rule, so the failure is enumerated here rather than left for you to find. This is exactly what does not exist.

  1. (01)

    NO CHECKOUT

    There is no purchase screen anywhere in FieldLux. No payment provider is connected, no payment code is in the build, and no card has ever been charged by this product. TossPayments is named above as an intention, not as an integration.

  2. (02)

    NO PLAN STATE

    No account carries a pass, a start date or an expiry date. Nothing in the app can tell a paid account from an unpaid one, which is another way of saying every account is the same account.

  3. (03)

    NO ENFORCEMENT

    Delivery mode has never run. The behaviour in the two tables above is specified and unwritten, so no account has been in it and none can be put in it.

  4. (04)

    NO START-DATE PICKER

    Choosing a start date up to 14 days ahead is a decision, not a feature. It has no screen and no code behind it.

  5. (05)

    NO REFUND ROUTE

    The refund terms above say what would be honoured. There is nothing to refund yet and no mechanism to refund it with.
