Taxes. Prices are in US dollars. VAT or sales tax is added at checkout where it applies, and you see the total before you pay. Businesses that enter a valid VAT or business registration number are not charged VAT, and every Paddle invoice can show your company name and VAT number. Your card issuer may charge a foreign-transaction fee.

Who sells. Payments are processed by Paddle.com, our reseller and the Merchant of Record for all our orders. Paddle handles billing, tax and invoices; we cannot issue Korean tax invoices. Questions or refund requests: jay@field-lux.com.

7-day passes. For a single job. A pass starts when you buy it and ends 7 days later, with no renewal and nothing to cancel. Buy another before yours ends and the new one starts when it ends. Need FieldLux for more than two weeks? A monthly plan costs less.

Refunds. 14-day money-back guarantee on your first monthly or yearly payment, once per account. A renewal can be refunded within 7 days of the charge. A 7-day pass, including one that has not started yet, can be refunded during its 7 days: in full if you have not used it, otherwise for the days you have not used, counted by day. See the Refund Policy.

Checkout is not open yet. These are the prices we plan to charge. Nothing is charged during open beta.

One person per plan. Projection and Pro are for one person. For several people who edit, see Team plans below.

Storage. Monthly and yearly plans: 10 GB for models and media together. A 7-day pass, the free trial and the open beta: 2 GB of imported models and 1 GB of media. 200 MB per file on every plan. Going over never deletes anything; new uploads pause until there is room.

Who needs a seat. Only people who edit. Clients and site managers who open read-only share links or read the PDF Tech Rider don't need a seat, and there is no limit on them.

Seats. One seat is one named person. Add a seat at any time and pay a prorated amount for the rest of the billing period; a removed seat stays billed until the next renewal.

Prices. In US dollars before tax. Team is billed monthly or yearly; there is no 7-day pass for Team.

When paid plans start

What your account gets.

Founding Members (the early testers on our invite list before open beta; the list is closed). Pro is included for 12 months from the day paid plans start. No card needed.

Other beta accounts. Pro stays free for 30 days from the day paid plans start. Nothing is charged unless you choose a plan.

Accounts created after paid plans start. 21 days of Pro, with every feature, free from the day you sign up. No card. When it ends, nothing is charged and your account becomes Viewer: you can open, share and export your projects, but changes are not saved until you choose a plan.

Buying for a company? See Team plans above. If you need an invoice or a purchase order, email jay@field-lux.com.

Short answers

Pricing FAQ.

Is FieldLux free right now?

Yes. Open beta includes the full current toolset, including fixtures, fog and the Play Dock. The Report Builder proposal PDF is temporarily disabled during the beta. No payment method is required.

When do paid plans start?

We plan to start them in mid-December 2026 and will post the exact date on this page at least 14 days in advance. Until then FieldLux is free.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Accounts created after paid plans start get 21 days of Pro, with every feature, free and with no card. When the trial ends, nothing is charged. Accounts created before then get 30 days of Pro free from the start date instead, and Founding Members get 12 months.

How does the 7-day pass work?

A pass is one payment for 7 days of Projection ($15) or Pro ($20), plus tax where it applies. It starts when you buy it, never renews and needs no cancelling. Buy another before it ends and the new one starts when the first ends. If you need FieldLux for more than two weeks, a monthly plan costs less.

Is there a plan for teams?

Yes. Team is $99 a month for 3 editor seats with every feature, and $30 a month for each extra seat, or $990 a year plus $300 a year per extra seat. Teams that join during early access keep this price for their first year. People who only view share links or the PDF Tech Rider don't need a seat. Team is not in the app yet: email jay@field-lux.com to join early.

Which plan has the Play Dock, Warp & Blend and the Working Plane?

Projection. Pro adds one thing: creating lighting, meaning fixtures, fog machines and volumetric beams. Lighting that already exists in a shared or imported project stays visible on Projection: you can view it, read its Working Plane and copy the fixture schedule.

What happens when a plan or pass ends?

Your projects stay in your account. You can still open them, share read-only links, export the PDF Tech Rider and download project files. Saving pauses until you choose a plan again: you can still change a project in the app, but the change is not saved to your account. Adding lighting needs Pro. We don't delete projects when a plan ends.

How does yearly billing work?

Yearly plans are billed once a year: $384 for Projection or $480 for Pro, plus tax where it applies. Cancel any time. Within 14 days of your first monthly or yearly payment you can get a full refund, once per account, and a renewal can be refunded in full within 7 days of the charge. After that, we refund the unused days of your year.

How do I cancel?

Open Account settings in the app and choose Cancel subscription. It takes one click and one confirmation. Your plan stays active until the end of the period you paid for, and you won't be charged again. A 7-day pass needs no cancelling: it ends by itself.
