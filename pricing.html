PRICING

THE PRICES ARE SET. THE CHECKOUT IS NOT BUILT.

This page publishes what FieldLux will cost and how it will work. It is not a store. There is no purchase screen in the app, no payment code in the build and no plan enforcement anywhere in it, so nothing here can be bought today and no button on this page charges anything. Every FieldLux account right now is open beta and free.

It is published this early for one reason. Somebody deciding whether to build a way of working on a tool needs the number before the workflow is built, not after it. Every figure here is stated with what it counts, and the arithmetic that put it where it is gets printed rather than left for you to do.

Published: September 6, 2026. Prices in USD, before tax. No account has been charged.

THE THREE TIERS

Prices in USD, before tax. There is a feature ladder and it has exactly one step in it: the stage and architectural fixture lane and the fog machine are in Pro and not in Projection. Which period is cheaper for the way you actually work is arithmetic rather than opinion, so it is worked out two sections down instead of argued for here.

The switch below chooses which period each card prints. With scripting turned off the switch is not drawn and every card prints all three periods at once, which is the same information in more space.

WHAT PER USER MEANS

A FieldLux account holds exactly one person. There is no team, no shared workspace and no seat management anywhere in the build, so per user is per account and each account is billed on its own. A project has one owner; other people get read-only access by email or by a share link. Four people on Enterprise is four accounts at USD 396 a month, and they still cannot be inside one project together. Anything that would make per user mean something else has to be built first.

WHAT A TIER DECIDES

A tier decides which lanes are open. It never decides how big a scene can get, and it never changes how a number is calculated. Every ceiling in this app is the same on every tier, and the one that matters most, 200 projectors, is a hard shader cap that no payment raises.

THE ONE STEP

ONE DOOR SEPARATES PROJECTION FROM PRO.

Pro opens the light that is not coming out of a projector, and the air it travels through. If your job is projectors on a building, Projection is the whole product and Pro adds nothing you will open. If you also hang fixtures, or you need to see a beam in haze, that is the step.

WHAT PRO OPENS

Stage and architectural fixtures with their own photometry, the isolux working plane and its target profiles, IES, EULUMDAT and GDTF import, the fixture schedule, and the fog machine with beams rendered through the air. In the code these are one door, not six: the fog machine's creation menu rides the same switch as the fixture lane, so an account without fixtures never reaches the atmosphere at all.

WHAT STAYS IN PROJECTION

LED walls stay, with their brightness in nits, their projection spill in lux and their washout rating, because an LED wall is a projection-lane object that happens to emit light. So does everything a projector job needs: the optics, the coverage, the blending, the warp and masks, the content, the measurement lane, the drawing lane and every deliverable.

A SCENE MADE IN PRO

Fixtures and fog machines are ordinary saved objects, so a scene that has them keeps them. On Projection that scene still opens, still saves and still measures its projectors, and the fixtures sit in it unrendered until the lane is open again. Dropping a tier is not a deletion event, and moving back up does not ask you to rebuild anything.

THE ARITHMETIC

EVERY CROSSOVER, WORKED OUT.

Eight comparisons on Projection and Pro, and three of them say buy the smaller thing. The sums are printed because a ladder that only ever argued upward would not need a table.

ONE WEEK OF WORK

9 as a week against 39 as the month. If a job takes a week and the next one is a month away, the week is 30 cheaper and nothing renews behind you.

FOUR WEEKS

36 as four weeks against 39 for the month. Four weeks is 28 days, so that is 28 days at 36 against a whole month at 39. Buying by the week is cheaper right up to the point where you need the other two or three days.

A WHOLE MONTH

Any month needs five weekly passes to be covered end to end, 31 days or 30: 45 against 39. From the fifth week the month is cheaper, and that is where the crossover sits.

A WHOLE YEAR, WEEK BY WEEK

52 weeks at 9 is 468. Twelve months at 39 is also 468. Buying every week for a year costs exactly what paying monthly for a year costs, which is deliberate: working in short bookings should not carry a surcharge. What the month buys at the same money is that it does not stop unless you stop it.

THE YEAR AGAINST THE MONTH

384 for a year of Projection against 468 in twelve monthly payments, so the year saves 84 and pays for itself in the tenth month: nine months come to 351 and ten come to 390. On Pro it is 480 against 588, and on Enterprise 984 against 1,188.

WHERE THE YEARLY PRICES COME FROM

Ten months' price for twelve, then the monthly figure on the card rounded down to a whole dollar so the card and the annual total agree exactly: 32 x 12 = 384, 40 x 12 = 480, 82 x 12 = 984. That works out at 17.9, 18.4 and 17.2 percent off.

PRO AGAINST PROJECTION

10 a month, 3 a week, or 96 a year. That is the whole price of the fixture lane and the fog machine. If you open them twice a year it is worth reading the week rate rather than the month.

ENTERPRISE AGAINST PRO

50 a month, or 504 a year, per person. It adds no feature to the app. If an invoice and a purchase order are not worth that to your company, Pro is the same software.

WHEN A PLAN ENDS

NOTHING IS DELETED. NOTHING IS LOCKED AWAY.

This is the part worth reading before the prices, because it is what makes a seven-day pass a sane thing to buy. When a plan runs out the account changes what it will let you do next. It does not change what it will let you reach. The working name for that state is delivery mode.

(01) WHAT AN EXPIRED ACCOUNT KEEPS

OPENING AND VIEWING

Every project on the account still opens and still loads in 3D. Projectors, lenses, masks, materials and saved measurement results are all still there to look at, on a screen, in front of a client.

A SHARE LINK YOU ALREADY MADE

A read-only share link you have not revoked, expired or password-protected keeps resolving. A plan ending does not close a link a client is already using.

THE PDF TECH RIDER

The PDF Tech Rider export keeps working. A document you owe somebody can still leave the app after the plan that produced it has ended.

SHOWING IT ON A SCREEN

Delivery mode adds nothing to stop you. The viewport is not blanked, watermarked or covered over when a plan ends, and no measurement label is hidden that was visible the day before.

YOUR FILES

Imported models and media stay on the account under the same quotas as before. Expiry is not a deletion event.

THE TIER YOU LAST PAID FOR

A lapsed Pro account is a lapsed Pro account. Its fixtures and its fog still render when it opens a scene; what stops is writing, not the lane. Lapsing and dropping to a cheaper tier are two different things and only one of them closes a door.

(02) WHAT AN EXPIRED ACCOUNT STOPS

NEW PROJECTS

Creating a new project needs a live plan.

EDITING AND SAVING

Changing anything inside a project, and saving that change, needs a live plan. What is already saved stays exactly as saved, and paying again returns the account to editing with the same projects in it.

(03) AND NONE OF IT IS RUNNING

NOTHING CAN EXPIRE YET

No plan exists, so no plan can run out. Delivery mode is specified and has never run: no account has been in it and none can be put in it. Every account today is in full edit on every lane, with no plan at all.

THE FINE PRINT

HOW THE MONEY WOULD WORK.

Written in the conditional throughout, because none of it is live. These are the terms a first charge would be made under, published now so that they cannot be quietly written differently once there is something to charge for.

NOT BUILT

No checkout, no payment code, no plan enforcement. The app today has one state, open beta, and it is free for everyone in it with every lane open.

THE WEEK IS ONE PAYMENT

A week is bought once for seven days and does not renew. No card is stored for it and there is no subscription to remember to cancel. Buying another one is something you do on purpose.

THE MONTH AND THE YEAR RENEW

Both renew until you cancel, and cancelling takes effect at the end of the period you already paid for rather than immediately.

CHOOSING THE START DATE

A week can be dated forward at purchase, up to 14 days out. Buy on the Monday for a week that opens on the Thursday, so a pass bought for a show does not expire the night of the show.

CURRENCY AND TAX

Prices are in USD and stated before tax. What is added at checkout depends on where you are, and the payment provider calculates it. This page prints no second currency and no approximate conversion, because your card issuer converts at a rate we do not set and cannot quote.

REFUND, NOT STARTED

A week bought and not yet started refunds in full.

REFUND, RUNNING

A period that has started refunds pro rata on the days remaining. A month cancelled on day 10 refunds the other 20 days.

CHANGING TIER

Moving up takes effect immediately and is charged pro rata for the days left in the period. Moving down takes effect at the end of the period you already paid for, so a lane you paid for does not close early.

FOUNDING MEMBERS

Early testers who verify their invite code keep founding-member standing, which carries 12 months of access once paid plans begin. That access is Pro, because Pro is what the open beta has been all along, and nothing on this page shortens it.

NO RETROACTIVE CHARGE

No account is billed for time already spent in the open beta. Whatever the first charge turns out to be, it starts on the day plans begin and not on the day you signed up.

WHAT A FREE ACCOUNT IS AFTER THAT

Not decided. Whether an account with no plan can still be created once paid plans begin has not been settled, and it lands here in writing before it lands in the app.

IF THESE NUMBERS MOVE

This page carries the date it was published, and the date moves when the text does. There is no mailing list to announce a change on and no form on this page to leave an address in, which is why the date sits under the title where you can see it.

NOT BUILT

NOTHING HERE CAN BE BOUGHT.

A price for something nobody can buy is a claim this site would refuse from anyone else, so the failure is enumerated here rather than left for you to find. This is exactly what does not exist.

  1. (01)

    NO CHECKOUT

    There is no purchase screen anywhere in FieldLux. No payment provider is connected, no payment code is in the build, and no card has ever been charged by this product.

  2. (02)

    NO TIERS IN THE APP

    No account carries a tier, a period or an expiry date. Nothing in the app can tell a Projection account from a Pro one, which is another way of saying every account is a Pro account today.

  3. (03)

    NO LANE GATE

    The fixture lane and the fog lane are open to everyone. The switches that would close them exist and are used for beta kill switches, but nothing connects them to a payment.

  4. (04)

    NO DELIVERY MODE

    The behaviour in the two tables above is specified and unwritten. No account has been in it and none can be put in it.

  5. (05)

    NO SEATS, AND NO TEAM TO PUT THEM IN

    Per user is per account because an account is one person. There is no team object, no shared workspace and no seat management, so nothing exists for Enterprise to administer.

  6. (06)

    NO REFUND ROUTE

    The refund terms above say what would be honoured. There is nothing to refund yet and no mechanism to refund it with.
