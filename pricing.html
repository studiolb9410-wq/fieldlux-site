Short answers

Pricing FAQ.

Is FieldLux free right now?

Yes. Open beta includes every current feature, including fixtures, fog and Play Dock. No payment method is required.

What happens when paid plans begin?

We will announce the launch and final terms before checkout opens. The prices on this page are the current plan, not a live purchase flow.

What happens when a paid plan ends?

We plan to keep project viewing, read-only sharing, and PDF export available. Creating, editing, and saving will pause until access resumes.

How does yearly billing work?

The displayed monthly number is the yearly total divided by twelve. The full amount is billed once: $384 for Projection, $480 for Pro or $984 for Enterprise.
