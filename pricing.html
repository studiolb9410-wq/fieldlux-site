Short answers

Pricing FAQ.

Is FieldLux free right now?

Yes. Open beta includes the full current toolset, including fixtures, fog and the Play Dock. The Report Builder proposal PDF is temporarily disabled during the beta. No payment method is required.

When do paid plans start?

We will post the start date on this page at least 14 days in advance. Until then FieldLux is free.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Accounts created after paid plans start get 14 days of Pro with no card. When the trial ends, nothing is charged. Accounts created before that day get 30 days of Pro free from the start date instead, and Founding Members get 12 months.

Which plan has the Play Dock, Warp & Blend and the Working Plane?

Projection. Pro adds one thing: creating lighting, meaning fixtures, fog machines and volumetric beams. Lighting that already exists in a shared or imported project stays visible on Projection: you can view it, read its Working Plane and export the fixture schedule.

What happens when a plan ends?

Your projects stay in your account. You can still open them, share read-only links, export the PDF Tech Rider and download project files. Creating, editing and saving pause until you subscribe again. We don't delete projects when a plan ends.

How does yearly billing work?

Yearly plans are billed once a year: $384 for Projection or $480 for Pro, plus tax where it applies. Cancel any time. Within 14 days of your first payment you can get a full refund, once per account, and a renewal can be refunded in full within 7 days of the charge. After that, we refund the unused days of your year.

How do I cancel?

Open Account settings in the app and choose Cancel subscription. It takes one click and one confirmation. Your plan stays active until the end of the period you paid for, and you won't be charged again.
