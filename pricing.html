Who sells. Payments are processed by Paddle.com, our reseller and the Merchant of Record for all our orders. Paddle handles billing, tax and invoices; we cannot issue Korean tax invoices. Questions or refund requests: jay@field-lux.com.

7-day passes. For a single job. A pass starts when you buy it and ends 7 days later, with no renewal and nothing to cancel. Buy another before yours ends and the new one starts when it ends. Need FieldLux for more than three weeks? A monthly plan costs less.

Refunds. 14-day money-back guarantee on your first monthly or yearly payment, once per account. A renewal can be refunded within 7 days of the charge. A 7-day pass is not refunded once it starts, unless the law of your country or Paddle's refund policy gives you a right to cancel. See the Refund Policy.

For studios and companies

Team plans.

Team. $99 a month for 3 editor seats, plus $29 a month for each seat after that. Yearly: $990, plus $290 a year for each extra seat. Every seat gets everything in Pro. In US dollars before tax. Team has no 7-day pass.

What a team gets. A team workspace where every seat can edit, a shared library of custom lenses, fixtures and models, seat invites and removals, project ownership transfer, and invoices with your company name and VAT number.

Who needs a seat. Only people who edit. Clients and site managers who open read-only share links or read the PDF Tech Rider don't need a seat, and there is no limit on them.

Seats. One seat is one named person. Add a seat at any time and pay only for the rest of the current period; a removed seat stops at the next renewal. Projects belong to the team, so they stay when someone leaves.

Team storage. 10 GB per seat, shared by the whole team for models and media, with 200 MB per file.

Team is not in the app yet. Email jay@field-lux.com to join early and tell us how many seats you need.

Enterprise. For 10 or more seats, single sign-on, purchase orders, a security review or a data processing agreement. From $3,000 a year, invoiced. Email jay@field-lux.com.

Short answers

Pricing FAQ.

Is FieldLux free right now?

Yes. Open beta includes the full current toolset, including fixtures, fog and the Play Dock. The Report Builder proposal PDF is temporarily disabled during the beta. No payment method is required.

When do paid plans start?

We will post the start date on this page at least 14 days in advance. Until then FieldLux is free.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Accounts created after paid plans start get 14 days of Pro with no card. When the trial ends, nothing is charged. Accounts created before that day get 30 days of Pro free from the start date instead, and Founding Members get 12 months.

How does the 7-day pass work?

A pass is one payment for 7 days of Projection ($9) or Pro ($12), plus tax where it applies. It starts when you buy it, never renews and needs no cancelling. Buy another before it ends and the new one starts when the first ends. If you need FieldLux for more than three weeks, a monthly plan costs less.

Is there a plan for teams?

Yes. Team is $99 a month for 3 editor seats and $29 a month for each extra seat, or $990 a year plus $290 a year per extra seat. People who only view share links or the PDF Tech Rider don't need a seat. Team is not in the app yet: email jay@field-lux.com to join early.

Which plan has the Play Dock, Warp & Blend and the Working Plane?

Projection. Pro adds one thing: creating lighting, meaning fixtures, fog machines and volumetric beams. Lighting that already exists in a shared or imported project stays visible on Projection: you can view it, read its Working Plane and copy the fixture schedule.

What happens when a plan or pass ends?

Your projects stay in your account. You can still open them, share read-only links, export the PDF Tech Rider and download project files. Saving pauses until you subscribe again: you can still change a project in the app, but the change is not saved to your account. Adding lighting needs Pro. We don't delete projects when a plan ends.

How does yearly billing work?

Yearly plans are billed once a year: $288 for Projection or $384 for Pro, plus tax where it applies. Cancel any time. Within 14 days of your first monthly or yearly payment you can get a full refund, once per account, and a renewal can be refunded in full within 7 days of the charge. After that, we refund the unused days of your year.

How do I cancel?

Open Account settings in the app and choose Cancel subscription. It takes one click and one confirmation. Your plan stays active until the end of the period you paid for, and you won't be charged again. A 7-day pass needs no cancelling: it ends by itself.
